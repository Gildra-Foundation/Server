  gates were absent.

## Remaining privileged read-only audit

The approved attempt is recorded in
[`2026-08-20-privileged.md`](2026-08-20-privileged.md). Root-only controls were
blocked by unavailable non-interactive sudo, so the items below remain open.

Before a mutation, collect redacted evidence for:

- `sshd -T` including every include and Match block;
- active nftables/iptables backend plus provider firewall state;
- listening sockets with owning processes and interfaces;
- relevant sysctls per IPv4/IPv6 interface;
- `mdadm --detail`, SMART data and alert delivery;
- sudoers, enabled administrative accounts and authentication events;
- AppArmor enforcement/profile state;
- scheduled backup jobs, destinations and restore evidence.

This pass may use `sudo` only after explicit approval. It must not install
smartmontools, change permissions, restart services, modify firewall/SSH, clean
logs or create a backup.
